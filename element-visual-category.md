# Element Visual Category

## 1. Overview

Dokumen ini mendefinisikan standar elemen visual untuk menghasilkan desain Instagram Feed Apple Store Malaysia menggunakan AI Image Generator.

Setiap kategori memiliki karakter visual, komposisi, pencahayaan, warna, dan arahan desain yang berbeda. Standar ini digunakan sebagai referensi dalam menyusun Image Prompt agar hasil gambar konsisten, relevan dengan tujuan konten, dan memiliki kualitas visual profesional.

### Available Categories

1. Product Showcase
2. Product Education
3. Campaign & Promotion
4. Tips, Tricks & Hacks

---

## 2. Product Showcase

### Description

Kategori untuk menampilkan produk Apple sebagai fokus utama desain. Visual harus menonjolkan identitas produk, kualitas material, detail desain, dan estetika premium.

**Visual Objective:** Menciptakan tampilan produk yang premium, elegan, minimalis, dan menarik perhatian audiens.

### Visual Elements

| Element | Visual Direction |
|---|---|
| Subject | Apple products, including iPhone, MacBook, iPad, Apple Watch, AirPods, and relevant accessories. Product appearance must accurately represent the selected model. |
| Action / Pose | Floating product, standing position, tilted angle, open display, multiple product arrangement, or natural product interaction. |
| Background | Minimalist studio, luxury workspace, clean architectural environment, neutral backdrop, or subtle gradient background. |
| Composition | Centered hero shot, symmetrical layout, close-up product framing, balanced negative space, and clear visual hierarchy. |
| Lighting | Soft studio lighting, cinematic rim lighting, controlled reflections, realistic shadows, and reflective highlights that reveal product materials. |
| Art Style | Premium commercial product photography, photorealistic 3D rendering, minimalist editorial design, and luxury product advertising. |
| Color Palette | Silver, white, titanium, space black, neutral gray, and colors that accurately match the actual product variant. |
| Mood | Elegant, premium, sophisticated, innovative, minimal, and aspirational. |
| Quality | Ultra-realistic rendering, high-resolution details, accurate product proportions, realistic materials, sharp focus, and professional commercial quality. |
| Aspect Ratio | 4:5 — Instagram Feed. |

## 6. Usage Guidelines

### 6.1 Category Selection

- Select the visual category according to the primary objective of the Instagram content.
- Use Product Showcase when the product presentation is the main objective.
- Use Product Education when explaining product features, specifications, or capabilities.
- Use Campaign & Promotion when communicating promotional offers or marketing campaigns.
- Use Tips, Tricks & Hacks when presenting tutorials or practical usage instructions.
- Avoid mixing multiple category styles unless the content objective requires it.

### 6.2 Visual Element Application

- Use the Visual Elements table as the reference when generating Image Prompts.
- Select visual directions relevant to the selected product and content objective.
- Combine the elements into one coherent visual direction.
- Maintain clear visual hierarchy between the product, supporting elements, and text.
- Avoid unnecessary decorative elements that distract from the main subject.

### 6.3 Product Accuracy

- Use the correct Apple product model and product variant.
- Maintain accurate product proportions, physical design, colors, camera placement, buttons, ports, and display characteristics.
- Do not invent product features, specifications, or hardware details.
- Use official product images as visual references when available.
- Do not alter product identity to fit an artistic concept.

### 6.4 Brand & Visual Consistency

- Maintain a premium, modern, minimalist, and professional visual identity.
- Keep the Apple product as the primary visual subject when applicable.
- Use typography and color combinations that support the selected category.
- Avoid visual clutter, distorted product details, and inconsistent lighting.
- Ensure the overall design is suitable for Apple Store Malaysia communication.

### 6.5 Text & Typography

- Use clear, readable, and properly aligned typography.
- Keep promotional claims, product information, and supporting text accurate.
- Avoid excessive text that reduces the visual impact.
- Preserve sufficient negative space for headlines and supporting information.
- Ensure all generated text uses the requested language and exact wording when supplied.

### 6.6 Image Composition & Output

- Default Instagram Feed aspect ratio: 4:5.
- Ensure the primary product and important information remain within the visible composition.
- Maintain sufficient spacing around important visual elements.
- Use high-resolution output suitable for professional social media publishing.
- Follow additional output requirements supplied by the Image Prompt request.

### 6.7 AI Image Prompt Generation

When generating an Image Prompt:

1. Identify the content objective.
2. Select the appropriate Visual Category.
3. Identify the Apple product and exact model.
4. Select relevant Visual Elements.
5. Combine the Visual Directions into a coherent image-generation instruction.
6. Apply product accuracy and brand consistency requirements.
7. Define aspect ratio and output quality.
8. Include negative constraints to prevent visual errors.

The generated prompt should be specific, coherent, and ready to use with the selected AI Image Generator.

---

## 7. Negative Visual Constraints

Unless explicitly required by the content, avoid:

- Incorrect Apple product models or product generations.
- Distorted product proportions and hardware details.
- Incorrect product colors or materials.
- Unreadable or misspelled typography.
- Fake product interfaces or inaccurate software screens.
- Unnecessary logos, watermarks, or unrelated brand marks.
- Excessive decorative elements and visual clutter.
- Low-resolution textures and unrealistic reflections.
- Incorrect product shadows and inconsistent lighting.
- Misleading promotional information or unsupported product claims.
